# Frozen BabyAI UnlockPickup method-development protocol v1

Status: frozen after the structural GO decision and before executing any method
on the 60 development archives.

## Purpose

Test whether SC-DCTA remains useful on a multistage procedural corruption before
opening an untouched UnlockPickup evaluation panel. This is method development,
not final paper evidence.

## Archives and stochastic cascades

Seeds 0–59 from `BabyAI-UnlockPickupDist-v0` produce one 18-memory archive each:
six possible source families and three ordered workflow descendants per family.
One source family is active. True cascade length rotates evenly among one, two,
and three affected descendants, so later workflow refinements may repair the
stale source binding instead of assuming deterministic transmission.

Affected descendants execute the source-bound workflow validated in the
structural audit. Unaffected descendants execute the mission-conditioned expert
workflow. Therefore every replay label is an environment outcome. Cascade
length, active source, unqueried labels, and hidden lineage completion remain
private.

The three stages receive preregistered harm weights 1, 2, and 3, reflecting the
increasing amount of prerequisite execution consumed before failure. Weighted
and unweighted recall are both reported.

## Evidence conditions and methods

Each archive is paired across complete, 33%-missing, and 67%-missing provenance.
Exact inference enumerates every compatible one-to-one lineage completion,
source, and cascade length. Source-confidence regimes and priors match the
frozen OpenDoorColor study. Replay budgets are 2, 4, and 8.

Methods are SC-DCTA, probabilistic and hard-source DCTA, ENS, source information
gain, source-then-DCTA, positive-only DCTA, static risk, random replay, a
known-source reference, and full-information hindsight. Random replay uses 50
fixed replicates. Privileged references are not competitors.

After replay, every method may quarantine three memories ranked by posterior
expected harm. Recovery requires removal of every truly affected descendant.

## Development gate

Integrity, executable-label, and complete-grid checks are mandatory. Averaging
the two partial-provenance conditions at budget four, SC-DCTA must beat random
weighted recall and must be noninferior to ENS on weighted recall and recovery
under a fixed five-point margin. Passing opens seeds 60–299 without modifying
the method, archive generator, priors, masks, weights, or budgets.
